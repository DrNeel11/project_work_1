What is the research contribution?

The candidate is revocable stability evidence coupled to view selection by expected deferral renewal: select a view partly for how many future region-inspection days it can avoid, while condition-risk and audit deadlines take priority.

Region memory → condition + rate + uncertainty → finite deferral Supporting evidence changes → dependent deferrals revoked Candidate view → predicted posteriors → expected deferral-days recovered Actual observation → update evidence → reissue or refuse deferral

Adaptive revisit intervals, Bayesian filtering and risk-based inspection are established. Originality of this particular evidence-sharing and renewal mechanism still requires comparison with the closest published methods. See the method, limitations and integration guide.
